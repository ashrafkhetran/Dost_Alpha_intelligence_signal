# Business model

## Hypothesis

An individual subscription may support curated intelligence for professionals who
regularly monitor markets. Team plans may later monetize shared watchlists, coverage,
and workflows. These are unvalidated hypotheses, not current revenue streams.

## Revenue options

1. Free discovery tier to demonstrate evidence quality and establish a useful habit.
2. Pro/Alpha individual subscriptions for persistence, deeper coverage, saved work, and
   evidence-grounded analysis.
3. Enterprise contracts only after access controls, support, rights, and reliability are
   ready.
4. Avoid selling ads or opaque sponsored rankings that undermine source neutrality.

## Cost drivers

Source licensing and ingestion; storage and compute; embedding/retrieval; model tokens;
PDF/report generation; payment fees; support; security, compliance, and operations.
Track cost per active user and per completed analyst task before setting usage limits.

## Monetization guardrails

- Never sell rankings or editorial placement without prominent labeling and a separate,
  auditable policy.
- Avoid claiming returns, predictive certainty, or investment advice.
- Make trial, renewal, cancellation, and refund terms clear before checkout.
- Include model/data costs in plan limits; use transparent fair-use rules rather than
  unbounded promises.
- Enterprise pricing is quote-based only after scope, support, and source rights are
  understood.

## Validation

Conduct problem interviews, test sample briefings with disclosed illustrative data,
compare proposed tiers, and measure recurring use. Record objections, alternative tools,
budget owner, and buying trigger. Do not use vanity signups as proof of willingness to
pay.
