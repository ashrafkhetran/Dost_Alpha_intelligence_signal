# Product strategy

## Strategic thesis

Start with a narrow, trusted workflow: help a user scan a curated set of signals, open
the evidence, save relevant items, and produce a concise briefing. Add AI synthesis
only when retrieval can cite its evidence. Monetize repeatable decision value rather
than access to a generic chatbot.

## Initial target workflow

1. Choose a topic or market.
2. Review a prioritized, dated signal with transparent scores and provenance.
3. Inspect primary-source evidence.
4. Save/watch the topic or signal.
5. Ask a scoped question and receive an evidence-linked answer.
6. Export or schedule a briefing.

## Product bets and tests

| Bet | Low-cost test | Evidence to proceed |
|---|---|---|
| Users value signal prioritization | Concierge weekly brief for target users | Repeat use and specific saved/acted-on signals |
| Evidence links build trust | Show source count, freshness, and claim-level citations | Users open, inspect, and rate source usefulness |
| Workflow-specific analyst beats generic chat | Interview and prototype SEO/founder questions | Higher completion and usefulness ratings |
| Professional users will pay | Price interviews and a clearly described pilot offer | Qualified willingness-to-pay, not clicks alone |

## Experience strategy

- Default to the user's chosen scope and recent, high-relevance items.
- Make evidence, freshness, and score meaning inspectable.
- Separate sample, model-generated, retrieved, and verified content with unmistakable
  status labels.
- Keep the experience quiet: one primary action, compact information density, no
  fabricated live status or vanity metrics.

## Success metrics

**Activation:** topic selected, first source opened, first item saved, first useful
brief completed. **Engagement:** weekly active target users, repeat evidence views,
brief completion, return to saved items. **Quality:** source coverage/freshness,
citation support rate, correction rate, usefulness rating. **Business:** qualified
conversion, paid retention, gross margin after data and AI costs, cancellations.

Define event schemas and privacy retention before collecting analytics. Avoid collecting
question content for analytics unless users are clearly informed and have a choice.

## Validation gates

Do not expand the source catalog before documenting rights and reliability. Do not launch
RAG broadly until citation and abstention evaluations pass. Do not charge until
entitlements, billing support, cancellation, and refund behavior are tested end to end.
