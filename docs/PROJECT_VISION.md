# Project vision

## North star

DOST ALPHA helps decision-makers notice consequential shifts earlier by turning noisy,
dispersed information into transparent, source-backed intelligence.

**Positioning aspiration:** "Bloomberg Terminal for AI, Business and Technology
Intelligence." This is a product metaphor, not an affiliation or a claim of equivalent
coverage, data rights, or financial-grade service.

**Tagline:** Signal Over Noise. Intelligence Over Information.

## Who it serves

- Founders and operators evaluating markets, competitors, and new workflows.
- Investors and analysts tracking technology and business shifts.
- SEO and marketing practitioners monitoring discovery and demand changes.
- Research and strategy teams preparing concise decision briefs.

These are hypotheses to validate, not a confirmed market segmentation.

## Product promise

For each surfaced signal, make it easier to answer: What happened? Why might it matter?
How strong is the evidence? What should I verify or do next?

## Principles

1. **Evidence before eloquence:** expose original sources and dates next to summaries.
2. **Signal, not volume:** prioritize relevance and explain why something is surfaced.
3. **Uncertainty is a feature:** distinguish fact, inference, and recommendation.
4. **Human judgment stays central:** no unsupported investment or business directives.
5. **Trust earns premium:** privacy, provenance, corrections, and honest capability labels.
6. **Useful by default:** fast scanning, accessible controls, and clear reading hierarchy.

## Non-goals

- Reproducing a general-purpose news feed.
- Presenting sample/demo data as current events.
- Promising prediction or guaranteed business outcomes.
- Building enterprise white-label/API capabilities before customer validation.

## Vision measures

Measure time-to-understanding, useful-signal saves, source verification/click-through,
weekly retained users, brief completion, reported decision usefulness, and correction
rates. Revenue is a later outcome, not a substitute for trust and utility.
